# Q2 Utilities v1.4.1

- Utilities now uses the Wheel Sensitivity set in Q2 Pod’s **System settings → Controls** across menus, tools, World Clock, and the world map. At the default 50%, one selection step takes 28 units of wheel travel.
- Reversing the wheel clears partial travel, and scrolling stops at the first or last item.

## Installation

Extract `q2-utilities-app-v1.4.1.zip` at the SD card root and replace the existing `.utilities-app/q2app` and `.utilities-app/q2-utilities-app` files. Keep `.utilities-app/tasks.tsv` and `.utilities-app/counters.tsv` to preserve your saved data.
